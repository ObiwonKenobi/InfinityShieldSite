September 2026 Threat Brief

A month driven by exploited edge and management devices, led by two Citrix NetScaler flaws and a CVSS 10 Magento remote-code-execution bug, alongside the largest Microsoft Patch Tuesday on record. Breaches came mostly from unpatched web applications and third-party integrations, and police took over the KillSec ransomware leak site on 30 Sep.

Top items

Citrix NetScaler: two critical flaws, one with unauthenticated root command execution

Critical · exploited

Citrix disclosed CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 on Sunday 27 Sep. CISA added both to the KEV catalog the same day. The first lets an unauthenticated attacker run commands on any NetScaler ADC or Gateway. The second is a memory overflow reachable when DTLS is enabled, which is the default on VPN virtual servers. Mandiant says attacks began in at least early September, in North America and Europe, and that the observed campaign is tied to CVE-2026-88772. It deployed a PHP web shell (WHIPSHOT) and a Python tunnelling tool (SLAPSHOT) and set the setuid bit on /bin/sh to keep root. GreyNoise saw a failed attempt on 24 Sep, three days before disclosure. Citrix says 88771 is also exploited. Sources differ on which flaw the early attacks used.

Action: Install the latest NetScaler security updates now. Disabling DTLS or blocking inbound UDP/443 helps only against 88772 and does not protect against 88771. Because attacks predate disclosure, check appliances for web shells and a setuid /bin/sh going back to early September.

StyleSmuggler: unauthenticated RCE in Magento and Adobe Commerce

Critical · exploited

Attackers inject PHP into Magento's template system and trigger it through a failed-payment email. Sansec recorded unsuccessful probes on 23 Aug and the first confirmed exploitation on 4 Sep. Adobe shipped a hotfix on 7 Sep. All versions from 2.4.4 through 2.4.9 are affected. Adobe tested the fix only on the August 2026 releases of those branches and publishes no fix for 2.2, 2.3 or 2.4.0 to 2.4.3. A successful attack starts a small Rust backdoor that connects to a command server and hides under process names such as chronyd. Sansec has no sign yet that the backdoor has been used. Other operators left web shells and a RAT on some stores.

Microsoft Patch Tuesday: record volume, two exploited zero-days, a wormable DNS flaw

High · exploited

The 8 Sep release was the largest on record. Microsoft's notes list 974 CVEs, of which 964 need customer action (104 Critical, 860 Important). Third-party trackers report totals from 966 to 973 depending on how they count. Two local privilege escalation bugs, both CVSS 7.8, were exploited before the patch: one in the Windows Update stack and one in Windows ALPC that allows escape from an AppContainer sandbox. Trend Micro ZDI calls the DNS flaw CVE-2026-69730 the spiritual successor to SigRed. It is one of 20 wormable bugs that allow unauthenticated remote code execution on most supported Windows versions.

Supply chain

  • Brevo (email platform)

    A flaw in Brevo's SAML single sign-on was used against 138 customer accounts. Trezor warned about 347,000 newsletter addresses after phishing emails went out from its own sending address, and BitBox and CoinTracking were also affected. Reported 9 Sep.

  • Dropbox via Lenovo ID

    About 5,000 Dropbox accounts were taken over through a flaw in Lenovo's email verification for Lenovo ID, a legacy integration. Reported 2 Sep. Only an email address was needed to register a Lenovo ID that could sign in to the linked Dropbox account.

  • BigCommerce / Ribon apps

    A compromised application key for the Ribon apps, tied to a Fastr system breach, was used to inject malicious scripts into a small number of storefronts. BigCommerce says its own platform was not breached. Merchants were notified from 17 to 18 Sep and the number of affected stores and shoppers has not been published.

  • npm

    On 7 Sep Aikido Security reported four npm packages published by one account within an hour, carrying a Shai-Hulud payload with the same hash as one used in the 19 May @antv compromise. The payload runs from a preinstall script. The sources do not say how the publishing account was accessed or whether other packages were affected.

  • AI coding agents

    More than 13,000 sensitive screenshots taken during agent-driven development workflows were found committed to public GitHub repositories, reported 29 Sep to 1 Oct.

  • Scope of this section

    No September npm, PyPI or GitHub Actions compromise on the scale of earlier 2026 incidents turned up in the sources reviewed.

Law enforcement and research

  • KillSec takedown

    On 30 Sep, in an operation called KillSwitch led by Hamburg police and prosecutors, authorities took control of the ransomware group's leak site and secured at least 110 TB of data. Three people were provisionally arrested and eight searches were carried out in Greece, Romania, Spain and the United Kingdom. A 16-year-old is named as the suspected main operator. The group is linked to about 1,000 suspected attacks, about 500 of them identified as successful, and the figures may change as evidence is analysed. Ten countries, Europol and Eurojust took part, with support from Bitdefender and Group-IB. Police announced it on 1 Oct.

  • Liquid Network (Blockstream)

    On 6 Sep roughly 4,000 of the 4,200 BTC in the federation wallet was drained after a bug in the Elements code let unbacked L-BTC be redeemed through SideSwap's peg-out. The attackers returned about 3,400 BTC (~$262.6M) and kept about 598 BTC (~$47M). One report says no keys or credentials were stolen.

  • Bitget

    On 24 Sep the exchange reported unauthorized transfers from a limited number of hot wallets and suspended withdrawals. The loss was first put at about $351.6M and revised to about $387.5M on 25 Sep. The attack method has not been described in the sources reviewed.

  • Research and disclosure

    A public proof of concept called ShieldCrash reportedly bypasses the patch for the Microsoft Defender privilege escalation flaw CVE-2026-69414 (ShieldBreak), which was fixed on 8 Sep. No Pwn2Own event or major conference disclosure dated September was found in the sources reviewed.

  • Certificate and trust failures

    No certificate authority or root store incident dated September was found in the sources reviewed.

What to do

  1. Update every NetScaler ADC and Gateway, then hunt for WHIPSHOT, SLAPSHOT and a setuid /bin/sh dating back to early September.
  2. Apply the Adobe hotfix to Magento and Adobe Commerce, and move any store older than 2.4.4 to a supported release.
  3. Patch Check Point gateways and management servers, F5 BIG-IP APM, Arista VeloCloud and Cisco Secure Email Gateway, then check for prior compromise, since patching does not evict an attacker already inside.
  4. Restrict management interfaces for Check Point, Cisco FMC and VeloCloud to trusted internal addresses.
  5. Apply the September Patch Tuesday updates, starting with DNS servers, domain controllers and the two exploited privilege escalation bugs.
  6. Update WordPress to 7.1.2, 7.0.6, 6.9.9 or 6.8.10 and check themes for directories beginning with page-.
  7. Enforce multifactor authentication on SaaS and consumer-linked accounts, and remove legacy sign-in integrations such as Lenovo ID where they are not needed.
  8. Rotate API keys and application credentials held by third-party apps and email or e-commerce platforms, and review which vendors hold long-lived keys to your systems.
