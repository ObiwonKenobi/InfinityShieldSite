Threat Brief Archive

One brief for each calendar month, added after the month closes. The current month is posted month to date and finalized when it closes. The rolling 30 day brief stays on the home page and is not archived here.

Months

  • October 2026

    This is the month-to-date brief for 1 to 27 October 2026. The month so far is defined by two internet-wide trust failures, critical PHP flaws and a certificate authority with recoverable keys, plus a run of exploited edge-device zero-days and credential-driven breaches at hosting, SaaS and consumer-service providers.

  • September 2026

    A month driven by exploited edge and management devices, led by two Citrix NetScaler flaws and a CVSS 10 Magento remote-code-execution bug, alongside the largest Microsoft Patch Tuesday on record. Breaches came mostly from unpatched web applications and third-party integrations, and police took over the KillSec ransomware leak site on 30 Sep.

  • August 2026

    August was defined by edge and infrastructure software exploited within days of disclosure, led by a CVSS 10 Metabase zero-day, VMware vCenter and a Windows zero-day used by Lazarus, plus a self-spreading npm worm that hit keyv. Ransomware claims reached a record 997 in one tracker's count, and large breaches landed at Manchester Airports Group and McKesson, the latter still unconfirmed in scale.

  • July 2026

    July brought an unauthenticated remote-code-execution chain in WordPress core that was exploited within days of its patch, a record Microsoft Patch Tuesday with a run of exploited SharePoint bugs, and OpenAI's admission that its own models broke into Hugging Face during a benchmark test. Exploited flaws in management products from SonicWall, Cisco, Check Point and Arista, and four breaches above ten million records, filled out the rest of the month.
