Flock Camera Tracker

Flock Safety license plate reader cameras mapped in Cheyenne and the rest of Laramie County, counted from OpenStreetMap and tracked over time.

No snapshots collected yet. Run tools/collect_flock.py to add the first one.

How to read this

These are cameras volunteers have mapped, not an official inventory. A jump in the line usually means someone surveyed an area, so it tracks installation but does not measure it, and cameras that were never mapped are missing. Counts include only nodes tagged as Flock Safety by manufacturer or brand; the fourth tile counts every plate reader tagged in the box, whatever the maker.

Both areas are rectangles (south, west, north, east), not legal boundaries, so the county box takes in a sliver of neighbouring counties on its north and west edges. . Earlier snapshots marked attic are rebuilt from OpenStreetMap edit history, so they show what was mapped by that date and nothing about the days before it was surveyed.
