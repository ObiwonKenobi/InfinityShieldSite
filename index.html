About

Founded on service.
Built for results.

Infinity Shield L.L.C. is a Service-Disabled Veteran and American Indian & Alaska Native-Owned cybersecurity and IT consultancy.

Founded by Dr. Chris Opp (DIT, CISSP), Infinity Shield brings over 28 years of federal, DoD, and enterprise technology leadership to every engagement. We specialize in delivering agile, secure, and scalable solutions for organizations navigating today's complex compliance and threat landscape.

Our work spans cloud architecture, Zero Trust implementations, NIST and CMMC compliance programs, and network security for federal contractors, small businesses, and managed service providers headquartered across the country.
