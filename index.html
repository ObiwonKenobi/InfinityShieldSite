About

Founded on service.
Built for results.

Infinity Shield L.L.C. is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned network engineering and cybersecurity consultancy.

Founded by Dr. Chris Opp (DIT, CISSP), Infinity Shield brings over 28 years of federal, DoD, and enterprise technology leadership to every engagement. Dr. Opp's background is in network architecture: enterprise SD-WAN, data center design, and routing and switching at federal scale. The cybersecurity and compliance work is built on that foundation.

We design and build networks that perform from day one and hold up under attack. Our work covers enterprise network design, cloud architecture, Zero Trust implementations, and NIST and CMMC compliance programs for federal contractors, small businesses, and managed service providers.

Services

What we do.

Network Architecture & Design

Enterprise network design, including SD-WAN at federal scale, BGP routing, MTIPS planning, DNS/IPAM, and data center architecture, with a track record across thousands of sites.

Cloud Architecture

Cloud design and migration across AWS GovCloud, Azure IL4, GCC-High, and commercial hyperscalers. From FedRAMP boundary planning to 20+ production migrations.

Cybersecurity & Zero Trust

Zero Trust architecture, next-generation firewalls, endpoint security, and network observability, applied to infrastructure that has to stay up.

Compliance & Risk Management

CMMC 2.0 readiness, NIST 800-53/171 remediation, RMF A&A, and SSP authorship. Built for the Defense Industrial Base, federal contractors, and organizations facing audit pressure.
