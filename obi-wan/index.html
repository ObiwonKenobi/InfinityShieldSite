What each agent does

  • Weekly threat brief

    Running · Mondays

    Researches the last 30 days of exploited vulnerabilities, breaches, supply chain attacks, outages and law enforcement actions, checks primary sources such as DOJ, CISA and the CERTs, rebuilds the rolling brief on this site and publishes it, with a New this week section showing what changed.

  • Monthly archive

    Running · First of each month

    Once a month closes, researches that month alone and writes it into the archive, which feeds the month by month timeline.

  • Morning brief

    Paused · Daily

    A daily personal summary to start the day. Switched off for now.

  • Heartbeat

    Paused · Several times a day

    Periodic check-ins through the day. Switched off for now.

  • Session tracker

    Running · As sessions finish

    Works through the Security Congress session list: plays each session video, completes the session evaluation, and keeps a spreadsheet of sessions, credits and what is left against the 31 Jan 2027 reporting deadline.

  • Job watch

    Needs a feed

    Watches job postings. Its numbers will appear here once it reports.

  • Game server

    Needs a feed

    The Minecraft server. Players, uptime and activity will appear here once it reports.

Where these numbers come from

The threat brief numbers are counted from the same files that build the brief and archive pages, and the refresh count comes from the repository history. The session figures come from the tracking spreadsheet and are updated by hand in this page's data file. Machines that cannot be reached from the cloud, such as the game server and job watch, write a small stats file on their own schedule and this page is rebuilt from it. Nothing here names hostnames, addresses or ports, or what any agent is able to reach.
